# v0.8.0: a lint that refuses bad diagrams

## TL;DR

`c2m excalidraw` now checks every finished diagram, whatever it came from (`.mmd`, element array, whole scene), and `--strict` refuses to draw or upload one with an error. `c2m lint <file>...` checks without drawing. Mermaid input stops producing the three faults that most often got through unseen.

## Why

An audit of 99 diagrams found 87 with at least one fault a human would catch in a second: a subgraph frame narrower than its box, a literal `<br>` printed in a label, a diamond too small for its text, an 8-box `flowchart LR` that is 1,000 px wide and prints at 9 pt. None of them produced a warning. Now each one is either fixed at the source or reported.

## Highlights

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| `c2m lint <file>...` | prints one summary line and the findings per file; exit 1 on any error; also `c2m excalidraw --lint-only` |
| `--strict` | any error-level finding exits 1, writes no PNG and makes no link |
| `--page-pt <n>` | page width for the font-size check, default 468 |
| Lint classes | a: node outside its frame; b: overlapping nodes; c: literal `<br>` or `\n`; d: unbound or floating arrows; e: text wider than its box; f: text under 8 pt at page width |
| Mermaid `<br>` | `<br>`, `<br/>`, `<br />` become line breaks; boxes grow to fit them |
| Mermaid `LR` | `flowchart LR` / `graph LR` are redrawn as TD, with a warning |
| Mermaid subgraphs | frames are fitted around their nodes, titles re-centred, bound arrows re-anchored after a box grows |
| JSON arrows | arrows leave a diamond or ellipse at its outline, not at the corner of its bounding box |

## Upgrade notes

Lint findings print as `warning: lint ...` lines, so existing runs still exit 0 without `--strict`. Sibling subgraph frames that end up overlapping are reported, not moved.
